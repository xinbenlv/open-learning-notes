02 · 选题与试样02 · Topics & samples方向、样板、标准Direction, examples, standards

A. 选题任务书A. Topic brief

记录用户问题、意图、现有内容缺口、独特贡献、来源可得性、主形式、下一步行动，以及为什么值得现在做。Record the user question, intent, content gap, original contribution, source availability, primary medium, next action and reason to prioritize now.

B. 代表性试样B. Representative samples

覆盖常见任务、难例、证据不足的弃题，以及你最在意的语言与事实错误。文字、核验页、图解、音视频分别验收。Cover routine tasks, hard cases, topics rejected for weak evidence, and important language/factual defects. Validate text, verification pages, diagrams and audiovisual formats separately.

C. 校准资产C. Calibration assets

保留认可样板、反例、事实标准、风格规则、媒体要求、转换路径和编辑记录，形成可复用规范。Save approved examples, counterexamples, evidence standards, style rules, media requirements, conversion paths and editing history as a reusable specification.

价值与成本Value and cost比较用户价值、产品关联、独特性、证据把握，以及调研、制作、审校、维护总成本。Compare reader value, product relevance, originality, evidence confidence and total research, production, review and maintenance cost.早期用可解释的相对排序；没有基线时不要捏造精确 ROI。音视频成本应包含后续更新。Use explainable relative priority early; do not invent precise ROI without a baseline. Audiovisual cost includes future updates.

通过条件：认可样板与发布边界，AI 能复现质量，事实可查、发布可回滚、指标可读。试样数量、阈值和抽查频率待定。Pass when: samples and publishing scope are approved, AI reproduces quality, facts are verifiable, releases are reversible and metrics are readable. Sample size, thresholds and audit cadence remain open.

新领域、新语言、新主载体或明显不同的内容承诺，先局部试样。已经验证的其他内容类型继续运行。A new domain of expertise, language, primary medium or substantially different content promise needs local calibration. Other approved families can keep running.

03 · 自动运行03 · Run the loop生产、品控、改进Creation, quality, improvement
步骤Step必须留下的产物Required artifact失败 / 数据不足时On failure or insufficient data
1. 选题与排序1. Select and prioritize候选池、意图聚类、现有内容匹配、优先理由与成本预估。Backlog, intent clusters, inventory match, rationale and cost assumptions.与旧内容重复时优先更新或合并；没有新增贡献就弃题。Prefer updates or merges for overlap; drop topics without an added contribution.
2. 调研与策划2. Research and brief任务书、claim → source 对照、来源摘记与核验时间、主载体选择。Brief, claim-to-source mapping, source notes and verification time, medium choice.关键来源打不开或不支持论断，补证或收窄主张；不能直接跳过。Replace inaccessible or non-supporting evidence, or narrow the claim; do not skip the check.
3. 创作与编辑3. Create and edit内容版本、媒体资产、自然语言编辑、字幕 / 文字稿、来源引用与关联内容。Content revision, media assets, natural-language edits, captions/transcripts, references and related content.事实底稿优先；中文与英文分别自然表达，不靠逐句翻译或虚构经历润色。Write from a shared factual base; compose naturally in each language without invented experience.
4. 品控4. Quality control证据与质量检查记录；必要时由独立审校上下文复查。Evidence and quality reports; use an independent review context where needed.未解决的重大事实问题阻止发布；风格缺陷返修；超出规范则请求校准。Block material unresolved factual issues, revise style defects, and recalibrate work outside the playbook.
5. 发布与发现5. Publish and discover正式 URL、上线版本与时间、内链、sitemap 更新、适用的通知记录。Live URL, revision and timestamp, internal links, sitemap update and applicable notification log.发布失败可重试但不能重复插入；线上有误时恢复已知好版本。Retry without duplicate insertion; restore a known-good revision when the live result is wrong.
6. 观察与决策6. Observe and decide指标快照、数据窗口、内容版本、变化假设、下一步行动与理由。Metric snapshot, data window, revision, change hypothesis and next action with rationale.样本不足继续观察；异常先诊断。允许“保持不动”，避免无意义重写。Observe longer when sparse; diagnose anomalies first. Keeping a page unchanged is a valid outcome.

四道质量检查Four quality checks

① 有用：完成内容承诺，提供新增价值。② 可信：关键主张有支持来源，时效和适用范围明确。③ 清晰：语言自然，媒体解释问题，辅助文本准确。④ 可用：正式页面可读、可访问，导航、播放与主要行动能工作。① Useful: fulfill the promise and add value. ② Grounded: support material claims and state freshness/scope. ③ Clear: natural prose, explanatory media and accurate supporting text. ④ Usable: readable accessible pages, working navigation, playback and primary actions.

让失败有正确的出口Give failures a recovery path

无证据 → 补调研；同质化 → 改题或合并；发布故障 → 重试 / 回滚；规则不适用 → 局部校准；权限过期 → 请求重新连接。记录原因，避免任务悄悄丢失或重复发布。Missing evidence → research; redundancy → reframe/merge; release error → retry/rollback; playbook mismatch → recalibrate; expired permission → reconnect. Record causes so work is neither lost nor published twice.

外链与分发Distribution & links

可自动准备Prepare automatically

生成适配渠道的摘要、配图、引用卡和 UTM；评估相关性；保存待发布草稿和渠道计划。站内专题、目录与关联链接属于常态发布工作。Prepare channel-specific summaries, visuals, reference cards and UTM links; assess relevance and save drafts and channel plans. On-site collections, catalogs and related links belong to routine publishing.

按渠道授权后执行Execute under channel-specific scope

第三方发帖、联系别人和付费投放需要明确渠道授权。未授权的渠道只准备待审草稿。分别记录计划、实际发送、取得链接、访问与有效转化。Posting, contacting others and paid placement require explicit channel scope. For unapproved channels, prepare drafts for review. Track planned, sent, acquired links, visits and qualified outcomes separately.

不把批量无关外链、为了排名买链接或重复铺站写进增长机制；Google 将多类操纵排名的链接行为列为 link spam。付费链接按平台规则标识。[2]Do not build the loop around irrelevant bulk links, paid ranking links or duplicate site networks. Google classifies several ranking-manipulation practices as link spam; mark paid links according to platform guidance.[2]

输入：域名 + 已连接环境Input: domain + connected environment

domain、站点 / 存储适配器、账号权限引用、受众与目标、内容类型规范、来源策略、发布边界、预算与抽查配置。只给域名可以开始发现和诊断，完成接入后才能写入。Domain, site/storage adapter, permission references, audience/goals, content playbooks, source policy, publishing scope, budget and audit settings. 持续状态Persistent state

站点档案、选题池、事实与来源表、样板、内容版本、发布记录、指标快照、实验与决策、例外队列。失败与恢复都可追踪。Site profile, topic backlog, claims/sources, samples, revisions, releases, metric snapshots, experiments/decisions and exception queue. Failures and recovery remain traceable.

输出：可验收的结果Output: verifiable results

上线内容与对应版本、质量报告、数据回顾、下轮行动、待人工处理事项。收入和增长只有在有真实证据时才报告。Live content and revisions, quality reports, performance reviews, next actions and human exceptions. Report revenue and growth only when supported by real evidence.

  1. 先选哪个站点试点？Which site should pilot first?

    选择内容价值清楚、发布链路可控、反馈可观察的站点；最终选择待定。Choose a site with clear reader value, manageable publishing and observable feedback; final selection is open.

  2. 模板与接入契约如何验收？How are templates and adapters accepted?

    用上面的文章优先默认栈起步；确认存储、导入、发布、缓存与回滚接口。界面语言与文章发布语言分别配置，不要求同步扩展。Start with the article-first default above; verify storage, import, publishing, cache and rollback interfaces. Configure interface and article languages separately; they need not expand together.

  3. 各站点真正的成功是什么？What is success for each site?

    为每站确认一个主要完成事件，并保留曝光、点击、使用作为解释指标。Agree one primary completion event per site; retain impressions, clicks and use as diagnostic metrics.

  4. 哪些内容类型先自动化？Which content families go first?

    分别确认受众、主题、形式、证据要求与样板。不能从一种文章通过推导出所有音视频都通过。Agree audience, topic, medium, evidence and samples for each family. Approval of one article family does not validate all audiovisual content.

  5. 如何验收、抽查与暂停？How do gates, audits and pauses work?

    约定试样覆盖、质量阈值、抽查频率、敏感主题规则和恢复条件。Agree pilot coverage, quality thresholds, audit cadence, sensitive-topic rules and recovery conditions.

  6. 自动操作的范围与成本？What are the operation and cost limits?

    确定发布频率、预算、并发、可自动更新 / 合并 / 退出的范围；分发渠道单独约定。Agree publishing cadence, budget, concurrency and permitted updates/merges/retirements; define distribution channels separately.
